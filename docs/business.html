Let's talk business

Community Calendar Connect already works, for free, in Linn County. If you're with a city, a county, or a newspaper, here's what working together could look like.

See it live: linn.communitycalendarconnect.com →
For City & County Officials

Residents already get the calendar for free: no cost to your office, nothing new for your staff to run or maintain. A partnership goes further than that.

  • Official branding, so residents know it's endorsed by your city or county
  • A direct line to us for changes, questions, or support
  • Priority when we build new features

We're still early, so terms get worked out one conversation at a time, not off a price sheet.

For News Organizations

Building and maintaining a real events calendar takes ongoing staff time most newsrooms don't have to spare. Community Calendar Connect already does that work, and it can live right on your own site.

  • Embeds directly into any page that allows HTML, no development work needed
  • Can be pre-filtered to just your coverage area
  • Gives readers something genuinely useful without adding to your workload

It's a way to keep readers coming back to your site, not send them somewhere else.

Why Residents Stay Free

Free means free, for residents, and that's not changing. Our business conversations are with the organizations that already make information official and reliable for a whole community: city and county governments, and local newspapers. That's who we grow with.
